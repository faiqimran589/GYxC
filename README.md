# GYxC
The Global Youth x Cancer (GYxC) platform is a student-led initiative designed to support child cancer patients and eliminate the social stigma surrounding the disease. 

Core Mission: Help young patients lead normal lives, fund treatment, and empower youth globally to lead local chapters and access oncology research opportunities. 

Founder & Research Focus: Features founder M. Haider Bashir, highlighting his vision, bio, and ongoing research into affordable oncology therapeutics derived from agricultural waste.

Global Network & Team: Showcases a team of founders and country ambassadors (across Pakistan, India, Uzbekistan, Saudi Arabia, and Kashmir) alongside an interactive map tracking 500+ volunteers across 6 regions.

Impact & Stories: Details cross-border initiatives, including patient family interviews in Saudi Arabia, toy/book donations in Uzbekistan, patient funding in Pakistan, and hospital collaborations in India. 

Features & Technical Setup: Designed with a dark-themed, animated gene background, dynamic counters, contact options, and direct donation integration via Hack Club Bank.
