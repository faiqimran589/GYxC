The Lab & Research Mission

  • The Routine. AirPods in, Spotify running, hunting through complex cancer academia.
  • The Mission. Extracting cheap, accessible oncology therapeutics from Pakistani agricultural waste — proving high-impact medicine doesn't need high-barrier prices.
  • The Endgame. Rewriting the genetic script entirely. Haider has fallen so far down the oncology rabbit hole that he predicts textbook definitions mid-sentence.
  • The FinalDose Connection. Staying on great terms with Jeff Liu and the team at FinalDose (a Y Combinator–backed company since 2025 working to destroy cancer cells via direct DNA target recognition). Haider keeps a battle-tested journal of achievements while aiming to fix the exact mutations that trigger the disease in the first place.
